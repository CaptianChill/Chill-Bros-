Common questions

How much does PPF cost?

It depends on the vehicle and how much you cover. Front-end packages cost much less than full body. Send the form and you'll get real pricing for your car.

Do you do whole fleets?

Yes. Tell us how many vehicles and what kind. Fleets are usually done in batches so you're never short a truck.

Will a wrap damage my paint?

On healthy factory paint, a properly installed and removed wrap protects the paint underneath. Repainted or damaged paint should be checked first.

Who will contact me?

The V Performance team will call or text you about your quote. You're never obligated to book.
