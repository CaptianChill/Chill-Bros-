Good to know

Do you offer financing?

Yes. Flexible payment options are available, including $0-down financing. Ask the shop when you call or come in.

Can you handle my insurance claim?

Yes. For collision and body work, V Performance can work through the insurance claim with you.

How fast can you get my car done?

Quick jobs like diagnostics and coilover fixes are often done the same day. Bigger builds depend on parts. You'll get a timeline with your quote.

What vehicles do you work on?

Import and domestic performance cars like BMW, Audi, Porsche, Corvette, Mustang and Supra, plus trucks, diesels and motorcycles.
